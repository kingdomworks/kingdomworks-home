Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KingdomWorks LLC ("KingdomWorks," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information related to kingdomworks.io and the KingdomWorks Agent Alerts SMS program.

Information we collect

When you opt in to KingdomWorks Agent Alerts, we collect your name, your mobile phone number, and a record of your SMS consent (the date and the method you used to opt in). We also keep a record of the messages we send to you and of any opt-out (STOP) or help (HELP) requests you reply with.

How we use your information

We use your mobile phone number only to send the KingdomWorks Agent Alerts notifications you agreed to receive and to respond to your HELP and STOP requests. We do not use it for marketing or promotional messages.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with the service providers that deliver our text messages (such as our SMS provider, Twilio), and only for that purpose.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only for as long as you are subscribed, or as long as needed to meet legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it from unauthorized access.

Your choices

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. To ask us to access or delete your information, email hi@kingdomworks.io.
