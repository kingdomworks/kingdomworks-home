Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chris Pawlukiewicz, an individual software developer (sole proprietor) ("we," "us"), collects, uses, and protects information for the Chris Pawlukiewicz Dev Alerts program.

Information we collect

When you sign up for Chris Pawlukiewicz Dev Alerts, we collect your name. If you choose to opt in to text messages, we also collect your mobile phone number and a record of your SMS consent (the date and how you opted in). We keep a record of the messages we send to you and of any opt-out (STOP) or help (HELP) replies.

How we use your information

If you opted in to text messages, we use your mobile phone number only to send the Chris Pawlukiewicz Dev Alerts notifications you signed up for and to respond to your HELP and STOP requests. We do not use it for marketing or promotional messages.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with the service providers that deliver our text messages (such as our SMS provider, Twilio), and only for that purpose.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only for as long as you are subscribed, or as long as needed to meet legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it from unauthorized access.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To ask us to access or delete your information, email chris@kingdomworks.io.
